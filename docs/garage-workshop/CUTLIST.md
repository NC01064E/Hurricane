## Sheet goods (4x8, 1/2")

Per face: **5 sheets** works, with almost no waste: 3 sheets vertical for the 138" fixed run (sheet edges on 180" and 132"), plus 2 sheets for the two leaf skins. Each leaf skin (41.75" x 79.625") leaves a 48" x 16.38" offcut; two offcuts butt to make the 14.5" x 90" strip over the opening, and the 6.25" side offcut rips into two 3" strips for the jamb packs. Garage face = mold-resistant board (5 sheets); room face = regular (5 sheets, +1 for soffit/hatch backing). Ceiling: 8 sheets.

## Leaf weight

Estimated finished weight **~156 lb per leaf** (23.1 sf face; frame ~1.28 lb/ft per 2x4; garage board 2.0 psf, room board 1.4 psf; 12 lb hardware). Plan figure was 110-130 lb. Treat 135-160 lb as the design load.
