# Build checklist

Work top to bottom. Items in **bold** are gates: do not buy or cut past them until they are resolved.

## Phase 0 - measure and decide (free)
- [ ] **Measure ceiling height at 3+ points along the partition line; use the lowest as H.** Re-run `tools/build_docs.py` (or use the formulas in `CUTLIST.md`) for stud, jack and leaf lengths.
- [ ] **Locate the opener centerline, motor, and the rail height where it crosses the partition line** (not necessarily centered at 9'-6").
- [ ] **Measure the overhead door height and how far the open panels and track extend; confirm the partition at 8'-0" is behind them.**
- [ ] **Confirm joist/truss spacing and direction** (ceiling board type) and what the existing ceiling is (fire separation to the attic).
- [ ] Slab moisture test (needed before G-Floor).
- [ ] Panel: spare capacity and distance to the garage for four 20A circuits plus the mini-split's dedicated circuit (115V 20A for the 12K 115V models).
- [ ] Identify the exterior wall construction and decide how to treat the two exterior walls and the exterior door (AUDIT 11).
- [ ] **Read the hinge load rating on the spec sheet; confirm 4 hinges per leaf (or heavier hinges).**
- [ ] Confirm the house door is a 1-3/8" solid/honeycomb-steel or 20-minute door with a self-closing device (verify against the current Florida code edition).
- [ ] **Call the building department: permits and licensing for the mini-split, its dedicated circuit and any panel work.**
- [ ] **Decide:** glass-mat vs paper-faced board; 12K mini-split confirmed by Manual J or installer (`SIZING.md`: ~10,500 BTU/h peak); CPX panels + 0-10V dimmers vs phase-dimmable panels (and the Caseta hub); threshold bar in or out; jackshaft opener vs soffit.
- [ ] Re-verify prices, especially G-Floor (\$229.99 vs \$379.99 seen) and the hinge set.
- [ ] Clear and organize the garage; set up the bin + desiccant + VCI bridge for the steel parts.

## Phase 1 - partition wall and hinged leaves
- [ ] Buy lumber and board per `CUTLIST.md` (41 x 2x4x8, 2 PT 2x4x12, 2 x 2x6x8, 1 plywood sheet, 5 + 6 sheets of board).
- [ ] Snap lines for the plate and the opening; mark the opener notch location.
- [ ] Lay sill gasket; set the PT plate; Tapcon ~32" OC and ends (hammer drill, 3/16" masonry bit).
- [ ] Frame the fixed wall as two screwed sections; frame the opener notch (trimmers, head, sill).
- [ ] Build and set the jamb packs (jack + king, plumb); hang the double 2x6 header with the 1/2" ply spacer under the top plates; screw the top plates to joists/blocking.
- [ ] Skin the garage face with mold-resistant board; mesh tape and setting compound; prime and paint; keep the bottom off the slab.
- [ ] Insulate the fixed wall (R-13) and skin the room face.
- [ ] Seal everything: foam/caulk at the plate, top plate, edges; backer rod + fireblock at the notch.
- [ ] Build both leaves flat, **doubled stiles**, Z-brace low on the hinge side; skin the storage face first.
- [ ] Hang the leaves on 4 NRP hinges each; check swing, clearance and the 1/8" gaps; adjust.
- [ ] Insulate and skin the room face of each leaf.
- [ ] Fit EPDM seals, astragal on leaf B, sweeps, barrel bolts (room face), gate wheels.
- [ ] Mount the rim deadbolt on leaf A at ~38" and through-bolt the strike into leaf B's stile.
- [ ] Fit the threshold bar; confirm the bottom bolts and sweep seal.
- [ ] Test: unlock from the room, swing both leaves; confirm the storage side shows no screws, pins or bolts; confirm the leaves cannot be lifted off.
- [ ] Move the workbench and tool cabinet in; hygrometer on; log RH.

## Phase 2 - ceiling, insulation, lighting, fans, raceway
- [ ] Insulate above to R-30; seal penetrations; leave the attic end of the raceway labeled and findable.
- [ ] Install the 2" PVC raceway (long-sweep elbows, pull string, firestop) while the ceiling is open.
- [ ] Rough-in fan brace boxes and panel/wiring locations; install the soffit and hatch.
- [ ] Hang and finish the ceiling board; prime; two coats of paint.
- [ ] Mount panels and dimmers per the decision on dimming; install the fans.
- [ ] Verify the soffit clearance over the treadmill.

## Phase 3 - flooring, circuits, small stuff
- [ ] Electrician: treadmill 20A, bench/TV GFCI, network/UPS 20A (and the mini-split's dedicated circuit if done now).
- [ ] Network enclosure on the right wall, raceway runs, Cat6 from the enclosure to the bench end and the TV.
- [ ] Lay out G-Floor: two rolls with a cross seam and the end filler from the offcut; stall mats in the lifting zone and under the workbench; shim and level equipment on the sloped slab.
- [ ] Water-heater drain pan and smart leak sensor; VCI liners, desiccant.

## Phase 4 - mini-split (spring)
- [ ] Manual J or installer sizing confirms 12K (revisit only if the load exceeds ~11,500 BTU/h).
- [ ] Permit as required; dedicated 20A circuit (115V for the 12K 115V models; confirm nameplate MCA/MOCP) and disconnect; pad and cover.
- [ ] Head high on the back wall, aimed across the room; condensate and line-set route.
- [ ] Commission; confirm RH at or below 50% with the hygrometer; no condensation on the steel.

## Inspections and final checks
- [ ] Smoke alarm(s) in the room; extinguisher near the exits.
- [ ] Fuel and gasoline stay on the storage side only.
- [ ] Walk the exit paths with the leaves bolted: house door and exterior door work, both unobstructed.
