## 5. Price flags

- **G-Floor Levant 7.5 x 17 slate grey:** the plan used \$229.99/roll. One retailer search page showed the same roll at \$379.99 and another at \$229.00. If \$379.99 is the Home Depot price, add **\$300** to Phase 3. Re-check at checkout.
- **Titebond sealant:** the case price (\$106.48) is \$79.86 more than the three tubes you need; buy tubes individually.
- **Stall mats:** \$359 at Home Depot; farm-supply mats typically about half.
- **Mold Tough vs glass-mat board:** if you switch the garage face to glass-mat (DensArmor Plus class) to honor the plan's own spec, the price is not on the list; the plan's own note says about \$25-\$50 more for the wall.
- **Surface-mount frames:** \$171.96 for 4 (the plan flags a ~\$107 alternative as unverified).
