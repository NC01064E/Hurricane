# Garage Workshop Conversion - corrected plan

Revision of the plan prepared 2026-11-19. Items marked **[CHANGED]** differ from the original; see `AUDIT.md` for why. Dimensions assume an 8'-0" wall until measured.

## Goal
Move the workbench and tool cabinet out of the bedroom and into the garage, and store steel mechanical parts cool and dry: RH at or below 50%, with steady temperature mattering more than cold. The conditioned room is also a gym and mancave.

## Layout
- Garage 19 ft wide x 20 ft deep. A temporary, screwed-together wood partition runs the 19 ft width, 8 ft off the overhead door.
- **Door side (unconditioned, 19 x 8 ft):** lawn equipment, fuel and other heat-tolerant storage. Fuel never goes in the conditioned room.
- **Back side (conditioned, 19 x 12 ft = 228 sf):** workbench, tool cabinet, steel parts, gym (treadmill, rack), seating and TV, 80 gal electric water heater, network enclosure, mini-split.
- House door in the back wall near the right corner, left of the water heater, swings into the house. Exterior side door in the left wall swings out. Both must be weatherstripped.
- Hinged section: a 7'-0" clear opening at the **left** end of the partition with two 41-3/4" outswinging leaves. The opening is off-center so the opener rail passes the fixed section, where the notch and soffit are easy.
- See Schematics 1-3 (`drawings/`).

## Partition wall
- Air sealing before insulation: PT bottom plate on a sill gasket, Tapcon-screwed to the slab about 32" OC plus ends. Foam/caulk every edge. No plastic vapor barrier (Florida inward vapor drive). R-13 batts in 2x4 framing at 16" OC. Everything is screwed, not nailed, and **no adhesive** on the plate (it would fight the gasket and defeat demounting).
- **Fixed section** 11'-9" including the right jamb pack, built as two screwed sections. Studs per `CUTLIST.md`.
- **Opening framing:** a jamb pack (jack + king) at each side, 3" thick, so 3" hinge screws go through jack into king. Double 2x6 header with a 1/2" ply spacer directly under the double top plate. No bottom plate across the opening.
- **Garage face:** fiberglass-mat mold-resistant board is the plan's own spec ("NOT paper-faced") **[CHANGED: the priced USG Mold Tough is described as paper-faced; either buy glass-mat or drop the rule - decide before buying]**. Prime and paint; keep the bottom edge about 1/2" off the slab (PVC trim at the floor). **Room face:** regular 1/2" board or OSB.
- **Opener penetration:** field-locate the rail height where it crosses the wall before framing that bay. Boxed notch (studs both sides, small header and sill), seal the gap with backer rod plus fireblock sealant. On the room side, a shallow soffit around only the protruding rail and motor, framed independently of the opener mounts, with an 18 x 18" plywood hatch on cleats and a gypsum backing, held by a few screws. Keep several inches of clearance around the motor and do not pack insulation against it. Keep fans and panels clear of the lowered box. **[CHANGED: check the soffit against the treadmill's front end; see AUDIT 12.]**

## Hinged leaves
- Each leaf 41-3/4" wide and `H - 16.375"` tall (79-5/8" at 96") **[CHANGED]**. Frame is 2x4 on edge; **both stiles are doubled** (3" x 3-1/2") **[CHANGED]**. Top, mid and bottom rails, a center stile and a Z-brace running **low on the hinge side to high on the meeting side**. R-13 inside, 1/2" mold-resistant board on the garage face, 1/2" regular board on the room face. Finished about 135-160 lb **[CHANGED]**.
- **Hinges:** four non-removable-pin ball-bearing hinges per leaf **[CHANGED from three]**, screwed 3" into the jamb pack. Pins cannot be driven out with the leaf closed.
- **Outswing** into the storage strip. The slab falls toward the overhead door, so the leaf swings downhill and gains clearance; an inswing leaf would climb 7/16-7/8" and bind. All bolts and hardware are on the **room face**; the storage side shows no pins, bolts or screws. The leaves can be unlocked only from inside the room. Enter by the house door or exterior door.
- **Seals:** EPDM bulb on jambs and header (stop it on the room side of the jamb for outswing), astragal on leaf B, adjustable bottom sweep, 1/4" gap over the bar. Barrel bolts, top and bottom, pull the leaves into compression.
- **Lock:** jimmy-proof rim deadbolt on the room face of leaf A at about 38", strike through-bolted into leaf B's doubled stile. Leaf A opens first, then B. Check that the deadbolt, barrel bolts and astragal do not collide on the room face.
- **Threshold bar:** a removable 1-1/2" flat PT bar across the opening carries the bottom-bolt sockets and the sweep seal. It is a trip hazard while in place; unscrew it only to move equipment.
- Swing arcs claim the left ~4 ft of the storage strip; keep lawn gear out of it. One leaf alone gives a ~3'-5" everyday passage.
- Build flat: skin the storage face, hang the leaf, then insulate and skin the room face.

## Ceiling, lighting, fans
- Replace the ceiling on the conditioned side (1/2" board maintains the garage-to-attic fire separation; **check joist/truss spacing**, 24" OC needs 5/8" or sag-resistant 1/2"). Insulate above to R-30 and seal penetrations before the board goes up.
- Lighting zones: bright 4000-5000K for gym/workshop, dim warm for mancave/TV. Four 2x4 panels (18,768 lm; three already meet the 50 lm/sf target) on surface-mount frames, two zones, a separate switched row over the workbench. **[CHANGED: the CPX panel dims 0-10V; Caseta dimmers will not dim it. Pick a compatible dimmer/panel pair; Caseta app/voice control needs the Smart Hub.]**
- Two 42" flush DC fans, one per zone. The gym fan goes over the cardio/stretch area, not over the lifting platform.
- **Network enclosure:** wall-mounted, right wall at the back corner, mounted high above fitting spray lines and out of the T&P discharge path, drip loops on every cable, UPS on a dedicated 20A. A 2" PVC raceway from the top of the enclosure straight to the attic with long-sweep elbows, pull string left in, penetration firestopped. No active gear in the attic.

## Air conditioning
DIY pre-charged single-zone inverter mini-split, bought last (Phase 4). Head high on the back wall, aimed to circulate across the room, not onto the bench or steel. Condensate and line-set route decided before the ceiling goes up. **[CHANGED/decide: 18K is likely oversized for a 228 sf room; get a Manual J. See AUDIT 10.]** Do not oversize to 24K.

## Supporting measures
Insulate and seal the overhead door as a buffer zone; hygrometer in the conditioned room; VCI bags/emitters and light oil for parts; sealed bins with desiccant as hurricane-outage backup; water-heater drain pan plus smart leak sensor; an insulation blanket on the 80 gal tank trims summer standby heat. **[Decide: the exterior block walls and exterior door are the largest moisture paths; see AUDIT 11.]**

## Phasing
Florida winter is the free-AC window: build November-February and let the mini-split land last, before the May heat.
